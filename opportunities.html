Space

Driveways, garages, spare rooms, yards, pools — space you already pay for.

$0–$50 low effort No face

Renting out unused storage or parking space

Rent a driveway, garage, shed or basement to someone who needs somewhere to put a car, boat, RV or boxes. The space already exists and you already pay for it.

First payment: Weeks to months.

$0–$500 medium effort No face

Renting out your pool by the hour

Private pools rent hourly to families, parties and swimmers who want somewhere that is not a crowded public pool. High revenue per hour, and the heaviest regulatory friction of anything in this category.

First payment: Days to weeks in season.

$0–$300 low effort No face

Renting your yard as a private dog park

Dog owners pay by the hour for private, fenced space where a reactive or anxious dog can run safely off-leash. Your ordinary back garden is the product.

First payment: Days to weeks in an area with demand.

Voice

Recordings, narration and speech data.

$80–$600 high effort No face

Selling voice recordings and voice-over work

Narration, commercials, e-learning and audiobooks. Your face never appears, and the barrier is audio quality far more than vocal talent.

First payment: Weeks to months.

Digital assets

Templates, presets and files that sell repeatedly.

$0–$20 medium effort No face

Selling simple digital templates and assets

Build a file once and sell it repeatedly — planners, spreadsheets, presets, resume templates. The only genuinely scalable thing in this guide, and the slowest to start.

First payment: Weeks to months.

Creator work

Content production for brands, including faceless formats.

$0–$60 medium effort

Faceless user-generated content

Brands pay for short product videos that look like a real customer filmed them. Many formats need only your hands, the product and a phone — no audience and no face.

First payment: Weeks.

Body & appearance

Hair, hands, and other appearance-based markets.

$0–$30 medium effort No face Adult-adjacent

Selling feet pictures

A real, established market with dedicated verified marketplaces. Genuinely anonymous if you are careful, heavily oversupplied, and surrounded by more misinformation than anything else in this guide.

First payment: Days to weeks if you sell at all.

$0–$30 medium effort No face Adult-adjacent

Selling male feet pictures

The same platforms and the same rules, but a structurally different market: far smaller buyer demand, far less competition, and a marketing problem rather than a supply one.

First payment: Highly variable, and typically slower than the larger market because buyer volume is lower.

$0–$50 low effort No face

Selling your hair

Long, untreated hair has a genuine buyer market for wigs and extensions. It is a one-off sale on a classifieds model, which means you handle the transaction and carry the risk yourself.

First payment: Weeks to months, and frequently never.

Research & testing

Studies, usability tests and paid panels.

Free to start low effort No face

Paid research studies

Universities and companies pay people to complete surveys, interviews and behavioural studies. Genuinely no-cost to start, works under a pseudonym, and the pay floor is enforced by the platform.

First payment: Days.

Free to start low effort

Product and user testing

Companies pay you to use their website or app while thinking aloud. Your screen and voice are recorded — your face usually is not.

First payment: Around two weeks.

Unusual niche markets

Small, real markets most people never hear about.

Free to start low effort

Paid mock juror work

Attorneys pay ordinary people to read real case summaries and deliver a verdict before trial. Free to join, and strictly limited by where you live.

First payment: Unpredictable — potentially months.
