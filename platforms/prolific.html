Platforms

Prolific

18+ only partial anonymity

Academic and commercial research panel. Participants complete short online studies at rates the platform floors and polices.

Visit Prolific

What it costs you

Fee Amount Notes
Cost to participants None —

What you keep: The full study reward. Prolific charges researchers, not participants.

Getting paid

Methods: PayPal

Timing: Paid automatically once a submission is approved. Participants are paid in GBP and/or USD.

Requirements

  • Must be 18 or over
  • Studies are allocated based on demographic eligibility, so available volume varies by person

What it accepts

  • Survey participation
  • Online research studies

Where it works

International, though study availability is heavily dependent on the demographics researchers request.

Opportunities that use Prolific

Paid research studies

Universities and companies pay people to complete surveys, interviews and behavioural studies. Genuinely no-cost to start, works under a pseudonym, and the pay floor is enforced by the platform.
