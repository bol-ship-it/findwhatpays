OnlineVerdict

18+ only Government ID required not supported anonymity

Paid mock juror work. Attorneys post real case summaries and you submit a reasoned verdict before the case goes to trial.

Visit OnlineVerdict

What it costs you

Fee Amount Notes
Signup or participation fees None OnlineVerdict states explicitly that there are no fees to sign up or participate as a juror.

What you keep: The full stated payment for each case.

Getting paid

Methods: Not verified

Timing: Paid after completing a case review.

Requirements

  • Must be 18 or over
  • Government photo ID verification
  • Must be a United States citizen
  • Must live in the venue where the case is being tried — this is the binding constraint on how much work you see
  • Webcam and microphone on a desktop or laptop for virtual mock trials; phones are not accepted

What it accepts

  • Online case reviews
  • Virtual and in-person mock trials

Where it works

United States only, and further restricted to the specific trial venue for each case.

  • Case volume depends entirely on litigation activity in your area
