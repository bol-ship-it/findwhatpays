Platforms

Etsy

18+ only partial anonymity

Large marketplace with an established buyer base for digital downloads — templates, printables, presets and planners.

Visit Etsy

What it costs you

Fee Amount Notes
Listing fee $0.20 per listing Listings expire after four months and must be renewed.
Transaction fee 6.5% of the displayed price plus shipping and gift wrapping —
Payment processing 3% + $0.25 when using Etsy Payments Rate and percentage vary by country.

What you keep: Roughly 90% of a digital sale before any advertising fees, once the listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing are deducted.

Getting paid

Methods: Etsy Payments deposit to bank account

Timing: On a deposit schedule set in your shop settings.

Requirements

  • Must be 18 or over
  • A bank account in a supported country

What it accepts

  • Digital downloads
  • Printables
  • Templates
  • Physical handmade and vintage goods

Where it works

International, in Etsy-supported countries.

Opportunities that use Etsy

Selling simple digital templates and assets

Build a file once and sell it repeatedly — planners, spreadsheets, presets, resume templates. The only genuinely scalable thing in this guide, and the slowest to start.
