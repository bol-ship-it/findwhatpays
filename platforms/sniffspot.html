Sniffspot

18+ only not supported anonymity

Rent your yard or land by the hour as a private dog park. No up-front or monthly hosting fees.

Visit Sniffspot

What it costs you

Fee Amount Notes
Sniffspot commission 22% of the booking —
Payment processing (US cards) 2.37% of the transaction + $0.22 per charge —
Payment processing (non-US cards) 3.87% of the transaction + $0.22 per charge —
International payout fee $2.25 + 0.25% of the payout, charged by Stripe Applies to hosts receiving payouts outside the US.

What you keep: Roughly 73-76% of a booking after commission and card processing. Sniffspot's own worked example: a $10 booking nets about $7.34.

Getting paid

Methods: Stripe direct deposit

Timing: Payouts are free for US hosts.

Requirements

  • Must be 18 or over
  • No up-front or monthly hosting fees
  • You must have the right to host guests on the property

What it accepts

  • Fenced yards
  • Unfenced land
  • Fields
  • Indoor dog spaces

Where it works

Primarily United States, with international hosts supported at higher payout cost.

Opportunities that use Sniffspot

Renting your yard as a private dog park

Dog owners pay by the hour for private, fenced space where a reactive or anxious dog can run safely off-leash. Your ordinary back garden is the product.
