Affiliate disclosure

We currently earn nothing from any link on this site. No affiliate or referral relationship is active. This page exists because that will change, and you should be told plainly when it does.

How we intend to make money

Find What Pays is a discovery and decision layer, not a marketplace. We do not take a cut of anything you earn, hold your money, or handle your content. The intended revenue model is affiliate and referral commission from platforms we send people to.

Why this cannot affect rankings

Every site says commissions do not influence their recommendations. Here is the specific reason it is true of this one.

Affiliate configuration lives in a separate module from the content records. The data structure handed to the ranking engine contains no revenue field, and the ranking module does not import the affiliate config. Payout is not a factor the ranking code chooses to ignore — it is data the ranking code cannot reach.

Platform comparison tables are sorted alphabetically. Where we recommend one platform over another, the reason is stated in the text and traces to a cited fact you can check yourself.

What we will not do

  • Rank platforms by what they pay us.
  • Omit a better-fitting platform because it pays nothing.
  • Invent or repeat unverified earnings claims to drive signups.
  • Hide the fact that a link is monetized. Monetized links carry rel="sponsored" and are disclosed on the page where they appear.

Your cost

Affiliate arrangements never increase what you pay. If a platform charges a fee, it is the same fee whether you arrive through us or directly.
